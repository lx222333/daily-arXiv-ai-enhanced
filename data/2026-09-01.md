### [2] [Parametric Multimodal User Memory: Storing What Captions Cannot Carry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28609)
*Bojie Li,Noah Shi*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 本文提出一种将"感知记忆"（如声音、面容等不可被文本描述的信息）内化到模型中的个性化智能体记忆方法，将感知回忆分解为"语义定位"（vision-language模型）与"身份提取"（专用encoder）两个子问题，以单inline token形式在生成时通过注意力读取，无需外部检索。实验表明该方法在跨五模态的PerceptMem基准上显著优于纯文本记忆方案。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有个性化智能体的用户记忆几乎完全依赖文本（转录与字幕+相似度检索），虽然能覆盖可用语言描述的信息（如"我的猫叫Bibi"），却丢失了无法用caption承载的感知信息（声音特质、跨年龄跨光照的人脸识别、语气疲惫程度等）。实测显示基于caption的强重识别器在可命名信号上的recall仅为专用encoder的0.11，在不可命名信号上几乎退化为随机猜测。作者认为需要为感知记忆建立独立于文本的存储与读取机制。

Method: 将感知回忆分解为两个互补子问题：(1) 视觉-语言模型（VLM）负责在上下文中定位所指对象（what & where）；(2) 专用encoder提取身份密钥（who），存储为单个inline token，在生成时通过注意力机制直接读取，无需外部往返。VLM与encoder单独使用均有明显短板（VLM跨年龄人脸recall仅0.54，而encoder达0.81；无定位的encoder在两人场景recall仅0.05），但两者组合可达到正确区域上界（0.96），并泛化到多说话人音频与视频。识别核心无需训练，可对任意冻结模型复现encoder的recall能力，且注册成本为O(1)。

Result: 在PerceptMem基准（12个域、1080个任务）上，感知身份识别受容量限制（capacity-limited），而精确事实受绑定限制（binding-limited）。结论是身份信息应存储在参数化记忆库（parametric bank）中，而事实信息应存储在文本存储（text store）中。两种记忆可干净组合：同时拥有二者的智能体不仅能记住用户说了什么，还能记住用户是什么样的人。

Conclusion: 文本记忆无法承载感知信息，必须将感知身份以参数化形式内化到模型权重中；通过VLM定位+encoder提取的组合架构，可以实现训练自由、O(1)注册成本的高效感知记忆，且与文本记忆互补而非替代。该工作为个性化智能体的多模态记忆设计提供了明确的分工原则。

Abstract: A personalized agent needs a user memory: a persistent model of who its user is. Today it is almost always text -- transcripts and captions retrieved by similarity. This serves the captionable half of a person ("my cat is named Bibi"), but discards the perceptual half no caption can hold: how a voice sounds, how a face reads across age and lighting, how tired someone sounds. We measure this loss across five modalities: a strong caption-based re-identifier recovers as little as 0.11 of a dedicated encoder's recall, collapsing toward chance on non-nameable signals.
  We instead ground perceptual memory in the model, decomposing recall into two subproblems: a vision-language model grounds the referent in context (what and where), and a dedicated encoder extracts an identity key (who), stored as one inline token read by attention at generation with no external round-trip. Neither suffices alone -- the VLM identifies cross-age faces at only 0.54 recall where a face encoder reaches 0.81, and an ungrounded encoder recognizes a two-person-scene referent at 0.05 -- yet together they reach correct-region oracle (0.96), generalizing to multi-speaker audio and video. The recognition core is training-free: it reproduces the encoder's recall on any frozen model at O(1) registration cost. On PerceptMem (12 domains, 1,080 tasks) perceptual identity is capacity-limited while exact facts are binding-limited: identity belongs in a parametric bank, facts in a text store. The two memories compose cleanly: an agent with both can remember not only what its user said, but also what they are like.

</details>

By localizing AI for Indian classrooms, our work bridges the gap between global LLM capabilities and regional educational demands. The code is available at https://github.com/lingo-iitgn/GurukulAI.

### [4] [STAGEET: Stage-wise Typed Edit Tagging for Grammatical Error Correction with Arabic as a Case Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28614)
*Wenjie Lou,Alaa Mamdouh Akef*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: STAGEET是一个阶段式类型化编辑标注框架，将Seq2Edit的监督信号重组为有序的类型化可执行阶段，每个阶段拥有独立的标签空间和中间语句重写机制，在保持竞争力的编辑式语法纠错性能的同时提供更可检查的纠错轨迹，并在QALB-2014上达到最先进水平。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的Seq2Edit方法虽然高效且具有局部可解释性，但其可解释性主要是操作层面的——编辑标签仅指明字符串如何变化，单一编辑词汇表无法揭示正在进行的纠错类型。作者希望将编辑操作扩展到纠错类别，提供更具语义可解释性的纠错过程。

Method: STAGEET将纠错分解为有序的中粒度类型化阶段序列；每个阶段从自身标签空间预测，重写当前假设一次，并将得到的中间句子传递给下一阶段。框架实例化为两种变体：(1)端到端共享编码器多头模型，带阶段特定适配器；(2)每种类型一个独立标注器的完全专门化变体。

Result: 在QALB-2014和ZAEBUC上的实验表明，类别感知的分阶段纠错在保持具有竞争力的编辑式GEC性能的同时，暴露了更可检查的纠错轨迹，并在QALB-2014上取得了最先进的结果。

Conclusion: STAGEET证明了将纠错任务分解为类型化阶段既不会牺牲性能，反而能增强可解释性，实现了性能与可检查性之间的良好平衡，并在特定基准上达到了SOTA水平。

Abstract: Sequence-to-edit approaches make grammatical error correction (GEC) efficient and locally interpretable by predicting edit labels over the input rather than generating a full corrected sentence. Their interpretability, however, is primarily operational: a label specifies how the string should change, but a single edit vocabulary does not always reveal the type of correction being made. We propose STAGEET, a stage-wise typed edit-tagging framework that reorganizes Seq2Edit supervision into typed executable stages and extends edit operations to correction categories. STAGEET decomposes correction into an ordered sequence of medium-grained typed stages; each stage predicts from its own label space, rewrites the current hypothesis once, and passes the resulting intermediate sentence to the next stage. We instantiate the framework as both an end-to-end shared-encoder multi-head model with stage-specific adapters and a fully specialized variant with one independent tagger per stage. Experiments on QALB-2014 and ZAEBUC show that category-aware staged correction retains competitive edit-based GEC performance while exposing a more inspectable correction trajectory, and attains state-of-the-art results on QALB-2014.

### [5] [RedLight-VLA: Models for traffic-rule grounding and behavioral emphasis in driving policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28656)
*Bala Murali Manoghar Sai Sudhakar,Sourab Bapu Sridhar,Sandipan Das,Rahul Ahuja,Meda Lazar,Ashish Garg,Pratik Likhar,Senthil Yogamani*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 本文提出RedLight-VLA，一种针对行为克隆视觉-语言-动作（VLA）驾驶策略的训练目标，通过轨迹导出的行为重加权（BR）和并行辅助（AUX）头来改善信号交叉口的规则驾驶行为，在减少红灯越线、降低停车线速度误差和改善位移指标方面优于基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 行为克隆的VLA驾驶策略在信号交叉口面对稀有的规则驾驶操作（如刹车、启动）时表现不佳，因为这些操作在平均轨迹损失中贡献极小，且融合表示缺乏对交通灯和停车线状态的显式监督。

Method: RedLight-VLA包含两个核心组件：(1) 轨迹导出的行为重加权（BR），利用旋转不变的纵向动力学和保尺度约简对稀有的减速和加速样本进行强调，且禁用时可精确恢复基线；(2) 并行辅助（AUX）头，在连续的后融合规则token中锚定交通灯和停车线状态，无需自回归语言生成或修改轨迹解码器。两者均无需额外的手动规则标注。

Result: 在20秒序列、5秒预测视野的精选评估集上，相较于完全相同的VLA基线，RedLight-VLA将红灯停车线越线率从7.3%降至6.8%，停车线速度误差降低12.7%，3秒交通灯切片ADE/FDE从0.274/0.964m改善至0.247/0.897m。绿光误停从3.2%增至3.9%，但BR与AUX结合缓解了单独AUX导致的更大增加（4.0%）。联合模型还改善了非交通灯ADE/FDE（0.268/0.956m至0.241/0.876m），在全部四个切片位移指标上均优于任一单独机制。

Conclusion: RedLight-VLA通过行为重加权和辅助监督的有效结合，在不增加手动标注或修改推理架构的前提下显著提升了VLA驾驶策略在信号交叉口的规则驾驶能力，且联合使用两种机制能取得优于单独使用的综合效果。

Braking and launching examples contribute little to averaged trajectory loss, while fused representations lack explicit supervision for the governing traffic-light and stop-line state. We present RedLight-VLA, a training objective that uses expert futures and automatically generated perception targets without additional manual rule annotation. First, trajectory-derived behavioral reweighting (BR) emphasizes rare deceleration and acceleration using rotation-invariant longitudinal dynamics and a scale-preserving reduction that exactly recovers the baseline when disabled. Second, parallel auxiliary (AUX) heads ground traffic-light and stop-line state in continuous post-fusion rule tokens, without autoregressive language generation or changes to the trajectory decoder. We evaluate on a curated set of 20 s sequences with a 5 s prediction horizon. Controlled variants share the same backbone, training data, decoder, and evaluation population. Green-light false stops increase from 3.2% to 3.9%; however, combining BR with AUX supervision mitigates the larger increase observed for AUX alone (4.0%). The combined model also improves non-traffic-light ADE/FDE from 0.268/0.956 m to 0.241/0.876 m and outperforms either mechanism alone on all four sliced displacement measures.

### [6] [DS-Lighting: Making Agent Harnesses Explicit for Data-Science Automation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28590)
*Fan Liu,Hao Liu*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: DS-Lighting是一个统一的harness工具包，将数据科学自动化代理的harness设计显式分解为数据、工作流、执行和评估四个可复用层，并通过统一的MLE-Bench风格任务格式集成多个基准，提升了端到端数据科学工作流的可复现性、可比较性和可靠性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: DS-Lighting将harness分解为四个可复用层：数据层、工作流层、执行层和评估层；将多样化智能体表示为可执行的算子程序，同时支持预定义流水线和自适应搜索；并将多个开源数据科学基准集成到统一的MLE-Bench风格任务格式中，提供共享的任务接口、沙盒运行时和度量协议。

Result: 在多种智能体、harness、模型和消融实验上的结果表明，显式的harness设计能够提升可复现性、可比较性和可靠性，同时减少端到端数据科学工作流中可避免的系统级故障。

Conclusion: 显式的harness设计是提升LLM驱动的数据科学自动化系统可靠性和结果可信度的关键，DS-Lighting为此提供了统一的工具化支撑，并已开源。

Existing data-science agents often leave this harness implicit, making results difficult to reproduce, compare, and attribute across heterogeneous tasks. We introduce DS-Lighting, a unified harness toolkit that makes harness design explicit for data-science automation. DS-Lighting decomposes the harness into four reusable layers: data, workflow, execution, and evaluation, and represents diverse agents as executable operator programs that support both predefined pipelines and adaptive search. We further integrate multiple open-source data-science benchmarks into an MLE-Bench-style task format, enabling controlled comparison under a shared task interface, sandboxed runtime, and metric protocol. Experiments across agents, harnesses, models, and ablations show that explicit harness design improves reproducibility, comparability, and reliability, while reducing avoidable system-level failures in end-to-end data-science workflows. Our code is available at https://github.com/usail-hkust/dslighting

</details>
